# Getting started

This section takes you from nothing to operating your radio from a browser. The steps are:

1. Check the [requirements](requirements.md).
2. [Register your station](register-station.md) with the QSP operator.
3. [Install the agent](install-agent.md) on the computer next to your rig.
4. [Configure the agent](configure-agent.md) with your station credentials and your radio.
5. [Run it as a service](run-as-service.md) so it starts by itself.
6. [Operate from your browser](operate.md).

{{#include ../includes/service-host.md}}

The hosted QSP service is not open to the public yet. These steps describe how it will work. If you run your own instance, see the [self-hosting quick start](../advanced/self-hosting.md) and use your own address as the service address.
